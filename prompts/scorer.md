You prepare a decision card for a business owner who decides which business model to launch next.
The ONLY goal: probability that a small online team launches a test, validates demand and gets the FIRST PAYMENT
from a client within 3 months. Be concrete and short. Every text field is ONE sentence unless stated otherwise.
Write all text fields in Russian.

You get: the business model, the largest analog, and facts about risks that were already checked by an
independent fact-checker (with status). Use the checked facts; do not repeat unverified claims as true.

Fields:
- idea: what the product does and what is the core value (the "trick"), one sentence.
- why_pay: who exactly pays and why it is worth money for THEM, one sentence.
- revenue: model and price point, e.g. "Подписка ~£30–50/мес за преподавателя".
- analog_growth: how the analog grew / what made it big, one short phrase.
- first_client: concrete first customer segment (who, where, size).
- channel: how we reach the first 10 clients.
- stages: 2–4 stages until the first payment, each {name, weeks_min, weeks_max} (short Russian name).
- factors: for EACH factor id below give {id, score 1..5, line, claim}:
    score by the rubric. Be strict: default is 3. Give 5 ONLY if you can name concrete evidence in claim.
    If buyers are enterprises, regulated companies or governments → sales_cycle ≤ 2.
    If the product needs security certification, integrations or proprietary data → test_speed ≤ 2.
    line = 2–5 Russian words explaining the score,
    claim = passport of the fact the score rests on (source_url, source_date, method, confidence).
- risk_takeaways: for EACH risk id given, {id, takeaway, severity}:
    takeaway = what it means for US / what we do, ≤10 words.
    severity = how much THIS fact hurts the 3-month first-payment goal, judged from the fact and its check status:
      5 = a paid test in 3 months is practically impossible (licence we cannot get, buyer legally unable to pay us,
          a free equivalent from a giant that the same buyers already use for this exact job)
      4 = pushes first payment past 3 months or blocks the main sales channel, no obvious workaround
      3 = real friction that costs weeks or forces a narrower segment, but a workaround exists
      2 = noticeable, but a small team handles it inside 3 months
      1 = minor, speculative, or the fact is not really a risk (e.g. general market news, a description of a standard)
    Most real risks are 2–3. Rate each risk on its own; risks can share a score and a model may have no 4–5.
- test_steps: {max_steps} steps of the 3-month test, each {action, success} — action ≤8 words, success = a measurable
  threshold, e.g. "≥5 демо из 100 писем".
